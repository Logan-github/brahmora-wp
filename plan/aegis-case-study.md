AEGIS — Operational Decision & Governance Platform
From platform visibility to governed autonomous operations.
AEGIS gives engineering and operations teams a continuously updated understanding of their cloud and Kubernetes environments, then turns that context into safer operational decisions, governed actions, verified recovery and continuous improvement.
What AEGIS Delivers
KNOW — Platform Foundation
Discover cloud resources, services, dependencies, ownership, configuration drift and infrastructure intent.
RUN — Reliable Operations
Unify operational signals, incidents, SLOs, changes and service health to understand what is happening, why it matters and what action should follow.
ENFORCE — Governed Control
Apply policy, authority, blast-radius assessment, approvals and execution guardrails before changes are made. Verify outcomes and retain evidence.
IMPROVE — Operational Intelligence
Use reliability, cost, anomaly, architecture and operational data to identify risks, improve decisions and progressively reduce recurring operational work.
The AEGIS Difference
AEGIS connects observation → decision → control → execution → verification → evidence in one operational system.
