The Problem
Modern operations are fragmented
Teams already have cloud platforms, monitoring, observability, automation, IaC and ITSM. The challenge is no longer collecting more data.
📡

Signal noise, no context

Alerts from infrastructure, Kubernetes, applications and security arrive as independent, disconnected streams with no unified situational picture.

🔀

Decisions without governance

Operational actions execute without consistent policy checks, authority validation or blast-radius assessment—creating compliance and reliability gaps.

🌫️

Automation without control

Runbooks and automation tools execute without pre-execution assurance, post-action verification or recovery readiness evaluation.

🧠

Knowledge disappears

Critical operational context—what happened, what was tried, what worked—is lost at the end of every incident, repeating the same cycles.

The real challenge is answering: What is happening? → Why does it matter? → What should happen? → Is the action safe? → Did it work? → What can we learn?

Governed Autonomy

Pre-Execution Assurance

Before any unattended action executes, AEGIS evaluates:

  • 🎯 Target identity & ownership
  • 📋 Required operational evidence
  • 📜 Applicable policy & delegated authority
  • 💥 Current blast-radius assessment
  • ✅ Verification capability confirmed
  • 🔄 Recovery readiness & rollback path
  • 🚨 Failure & escalation path defined
"Failure to establish a required condition can only maintain or reduce autonomy. It can never increase it."

Autonomy is Progressive

AEGIS does not treat autonomy as simply automation on or off. Organisations define precisely what, where, and when actions are permitted.
